PHASE 3: BUILD

From Learning to Creating

Phase 3 is where you apply everything you've learned. Working in teams with fellow builders, you'll collaborate on real projects, ship actual products, and gain experience that goes beyond tutorials and exercises.

  • Form cross-functional teams with builders in complementary roles
  • Work on real-world projects with actual users and impact
  • Experience product development workflows and team collaboration
  • Ship projects and build a professional portfolio through execution
  • Get feedback and mentorship as you navigate team dynamics
  • Build meaningful relationships and professional network in tech

What to Expect

Phase 3 combines team project work with professional development. You'll experience real product cycles, collaborate across different specialties, and ship projects that demonstrate your capabilities to potential employers or clients. This phase bridges the gap between learning and professional practice.

Team Projects

Collaborate with builders across different roles to create real projects with actual scope and impact.

Product Shipping

Go through the full product development cycle and deploy your work to real users.

Professional Skills

Learn communication, project management, and collaboration skills essential in tech teams.

Portfolio Building

Create shipped projects that demonstrate your skills to employers or clients.

Who Should Apply

Phase 3 is for builders who've completed Phase 2 or have equivalent foundational knowledge and are ready to work in teams. You should be comfortable with your technical skills and eager to apply them in real collaboration with others. This phase requires commitment to your team and project for 3-4 months.
