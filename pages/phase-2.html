PHASE 2: DEVELOP

From Discovery to Execution

Phase 2 builds on the career clarity you gained in Phase 1. This phase focuses on structured skill development, guided learning paths, and accountability systems designed to help you:

  • Follow curated learning roadmaps tailored to your chosen tech path
  • Work through hands-on projects that build real portfolio work
  • Establish consistent learning habits and accountability partnerships
  • Understand how to leverage AI tools in your learning workflow
  • Get mentorship and guidance from experienced builders and practitioners
  • Connect with cohort members on the same learning journey

What to Expect

Phase 2 combines self-paced learning modules with regular group check-ins, project milestones, and peer accountability. You'll work on assignments that build practical skills while developing the discipline and systems needed for long-term success in tech.

Structured Learning

Curated resources and learning paths designed by industry professionals for your chosen focus area.

Hands-On Projects

Build real projects that you can showcase in your portfolio and discuss in interviews.

Accountability Systems

Weekly check-ins and peer accountability partnerships to keep you consistent and motivated.

Mentorship & Support

Direct access to experienced mentors who guide your progress and answer your questions.

Ideal For

Phase 2 is perfect if you've completed Phase 1 and are ready to commit to 8-12 weeks of focused skill development. Whether you're transitioning careers, filling knowledge gaps, or building depth in your chosen area, this phase provides the structure and support you need.
